FAQ

Questions, answered

What do I need to start?

Just a Google Maps link of the business, or a Google Sheet of leads. During the free beta you also need a free Google Gemini API key (takes 2 minutes, no card).

Does PinPage invent information?

No. Phone, address, rating and map come from the listing. Facts like opening hours, prices or reviews are never made up; you can add them in one click from the Google listing.

Can the business owner use the website for real?

Yes. Once they approve, the demo label is removed and it can be connected to their own domain.

Can a business ask us to remove a demo?

Always. Every demo has a “Remove” link, and demos are hidden from search engines.
